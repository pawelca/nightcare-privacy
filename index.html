NightCare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

NightCare is a logging app for parents and caregivers. This policy explains how NightCare handles information. The current Android release uses the package name io.github.pawelca.nightcare.

Information you enter

NightCare stores child profiles, including a name, birth date and optional gender. You can use a nickname. It also stores feeding times, feeding side or bottle volume, sleep and wake times, diaper entries and notes, growth measurements and notes, and feeding-mode preferences. NightCare uses these records to display your history, calculate summaries and provide simple activity hints on your device.

NightCare also stores app preferences, the selected child, installation and rating-prompt dates, and any saved premium status. Preferences use local app storage. Some installation information may use the platform's secure storage.

Local storage and security

Child profiles and care records are stored in NightCare's private app directory. NightCare has no developer-operated account system, synchronization server or analytics backend. The developer does not receive a copy of your child profiles or care records through normal logging.

Your device's operating system protects the app's private storage. NightCare does not provide a separate app password or encrypt the care database with its own password. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep its software up to date.

Device backups, transfer and deletion

Depending on your device, platform and backup settings, the operating system may back up or transfer app data through its own services. Those services are governed by the platform provider's terms and your settings. A device backup is separate from a NightCare account or developer-operated cloud service.

Records remain in app storage until you delete them or remove the app's stored data. The free history window limits which dates can be viewed; it does not automatically delete older records. To delete a child and that child's associated records, open Settings, Manage Children, and choose Delete for the profile. On Android, clearing NightCare's app storage removes its current local data. Uninstalling may remove local data, but a platform backup may later restore it. Manage existing backups separately in your device or backup provider's settings.

The developer cannot view, recover or delete a care database stored only on your device. Device loss or a reset can cause data loss if no usable platform backup exists.

Platform services and optional sharing

NightCare includes app-store libraries for native ratings and purchase support. These libraries can communicate with Google Play or the Apple App Store. Store services process their own requests under their providers' terms and privacy policies. The current free Android release does not offer a premium purchase. NightCare does not receive or store payment-card details.

If you submit a rating or review, the app store handles it. A public review can be visible to others; feedback submitted through a Google Play closed test can be visible to the developer. You can manage or delete a review through the relevant app-store account. Do not include a child's private information in a review.

The Share NightCare screen shares the app's download link and a general message, not a child's care records. The QR code on that screen also contains only the download link. When you choose a recipient app in a system share sheet, that app handles what you share under its own terms.

Some NightCare versions offer PDF reports. If you use a version with reports, you choose whether and where to save, print or share a report containing care records. The report generator may download font files; the font host receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, but NightCare does not send care records with the font request. PDF reports are hidden in the current free Android release.

Advertising and children

NightCare does not include advertising or an advertising or analytics SDK. It does not use your records for advertising. NightCare is intended for adult parents and caregivers, rather than for children to use independently. Information about a child is entered and controlled by the caregiver.
